Client Guidelines

Booking Policies

Please review our salon guidelines to ensure a seamless and punctual experience for all guests.

Appointments & Deposits

Advance booking is highly recommended to secure your desired time slot. A R100 non-refundable deposit is required at booking to confirm your appointment, which goes toward your final bill.

Punctuality & Late Arrivals

To keep appointments on schedule for all guests, arriving late may necessitate shortening your service duration. A R30 late fee applies for arrivals past the grace period.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

If you need to change or cancel your visit, kindly notify us at least 24 hours in advance to transfer your deposit to a rescheduled date.

Have questions regarding your upcoming appointment? Contact our reception team directly for assistance.

Common Questions

Pricing FAQs

Everything you need to know about our service rates, deposits, and custom quotes.

Why do some prices vary?

Pricing varies based on several personalized factors, including your hair’s length, thickness, desired style complexity, and the specific quantity of high-end products required to achieve your look seamlessly.

Do you offer custom quotes?

Yes, absolutely. We offer complimentary 1-on-1 consultations where our senior stylists analyze your hair goals and provide a precise, transparent quotation before starting any service.

Are deposits required for bookings?

A non-refundable R100 deposit is required to secure your appointment time. This deposit goes directly toward your final bill on the day of your service.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, major credit and debit cards, and instant EFT payments upon completion of your salon visit.
